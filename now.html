framed@ · framedat.uk

Client work · Ticketing & archive platform

A ticketing and event-archive platform for framed@ Limited, a Northern UK arts and music promoter. I designed the preview site and built the backend myself in FastAPI.

  • Passwordless auth — magic links and 6-digit codes via Resend; tokens are SHA-256 hashed, single-use, 5-minute TTL, rate-limited per email and IP.
  • Stripe ticketing — hosted checkout, signed idempotent webhooks, QR-code tickets, and a per-event door scanner where a conditional UPDATE guarantees one admission even under concurrent scans.
  • Direct-to-R2 uploads — browsers PUT multi-GB videos straight to Cloudflare R2 via presigned SigV4 URLs, so large media never touches the app server.
  • Video pipeline — phone-recorded MP4s keep their moov atom at the end of the file, which broke seeking; an ffmpeg +faststart background task re-muxes uploads in place.
  • 52+ pytest tests — feature-complete codebase; next step is the first Alembic migration and deployment artifacts.

diam Enterprise Suite

diam Systems · Enterprise products

Enterprise software we build and sell at diam Systems — all sharing a house style of vanilla frontends, hashed tokens, and org-scoped data.

  • Enterprise Messenger — end-to-end-encrypted team messaging for businesses. RSA-OAEP keypairs generated in the browser, private keys encrypted client-side, channel key epochs with fill-once member slots, and an organisation escrow key for recovery. Postgres RLS does the tenant isolation.
  • Helpdesk — IT support ticketing for businesses. Full ticket lifecycle, SLA targets, FTS5 search, magic-byte attachment checks, Argon2 + MFA, and seeded demo data.

Hard problems I have solved recently

Engineering depth
  • MP4s that would not seek — Cloudflare R2 serves raw bytes with no transmuxing, and phone recordings store the moov index at the end. Fixed by re-muxing with a stream copy, no re-encode, run as a background task after upload.
  • Concurrent door scans — ticket admission uses a conditional UPDATE so two scanners hitting the same QR at once still admit only one person.
  • Key rotation in E2EE — removing a messenger member bumps the channel key epoch atomically, with fill-once slots preventing key substitution.
  • Real tenant isolation — a ContextVar-driven PostgreSQL RLS layer where even a misconfigured query cannot leak another tenant's rows.
